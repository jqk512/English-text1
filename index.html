Unit 1

图 1、2；答案图 5 · 9 句
  1. The text defines “engineering” in Paragraph 1 as applying scientific principles to design or develop structures, while considering their intended function.

    文中第1段将“工程学”定义为运用科学原理来设计或开发结构，同时考虑这些结构的预期功能。

  2. According to Paragraph 2, early military engineers designed devices like catapults, while civil engineers focused on infrastructure.

    根据第2段，早期军事工程师设计了投石机等装置，而土木工程师则专注于基础设施。

    答案图写作 “civil engineer”；原句需与 “military engineers” 对应，此处采用复数 “civil engineers”。

  3. According to Paragraph 3, standards of engineering practice are maintained by professional societies, reflecting engineers’ responsibility to the public.

    根据第3段，工程实践标准由专业学会维护，这体现了工程师对公众的责任。

  4. In Paragraph 4, materials are selected based on their properties, such as the ability to insulate or conduct electricity.

    第4段指出，选用材料要依据其特性，例如绝缘或导电的能力。

  5. Paragraph 5 contrasts scientists who add to the store of knowledge with engineers who bring this knowledge to bear on practical problems.

    第5段将扩充知识储备的科学家与把这些知识用于实际问题的工程师作对比。

  6. In Paragraph 6, achieving an engineering solution often involves trade-offs between economics of operation and safety.

    第6段指出，获得工程解决方案通常需要在运行经济性与安全性之间权衡。

  7. The creative synthesis process in Paragraph 7 requires engineers to integrate conflicting ideas into an optimum solution.

    第7段的创造性综合过程要求工程师将相互冲突的想法整合为一个最优解。

  8. Paragraph 8 emphasizes that engineers must take many factors into account, even when their preliminary decision seems straightforward.

    第8段强调，即使初步决定看起来很简单，工程师也必须考虑多种因素。

  9. Paragraph 9 lists research as the most science-focused engineering function, followed by development and design.

    第9段将研究列为最侧重科学的工程活动，其后是开发和设计。

Unit 2

图 3、4；答案图 6 · 11 句
  1. As introduced in Paragraph 1, research and development has become a universal watchword in modern industrialized nations.

    如第1段所述，研究与开发已成为现代工业化国家普遍使用的口号。

  2. According to Paragraph 2, most innovations follow a path that begins in the laboratory and ends with market introduction, passing through a critical phase of pilot or prototype production.

    根据第2段，大多数创新遵循一条从实验室起步、以市场推广结束的路径，其间会经过试验性或原型生产这一关键阶段。

  3. The text defines basic research in Paragraph 3 as a pursuit driven by the desire to unravel the secrets of nature, often without immediate practical goals.

    第3段把基础研究定义为一种出于揭示自然奥秘的愿望而进行的探索，通常没有直接的实际目标。

  4. In contrast, as explained in Paragraph 4, applied research is specifically directed to meet a specific need by exploiting the findings of basic research.

    相比之下，第4段解释说，应用研究专门利用基础研究成果来满足特定需求。

  5. Paragraph 5 describes the first organized attempt to harness scientific skill to communal needs as occurring in the 1790s in France.

    第5段指出，首次有组织地将科学技能用于共同体需求的尝试发生于18世纪90年代的法国。

  6. Paragraph 6 reveals that World War I exposed critical weaknesses in technology and organization, prompting governments to establish new bodies with the task of stimulating and coordinating the scientific war effort.

    第6段表明，第一次世界大战暴露了技术和组织方面的严重弱点，促使各国政府设立新机构，负责激励并协调战争中的科学工作。

  7. Following World War I, as described in Paragraph 7, larger companies embarked on ambitious plans to establish their own laboratories, leading to a decade of remarkable technical progress where devices like the radio receiver evolved into reliable mechanisms.

    第一次世界大战后，如第7段所述，规模较大的公司启动了建立自有实验室的雄心勃勃的计划，从而带来了十年显著的技术进步；在此期间，收音机等设备逐渐发展为可靠的装置。

  8. Paragraph 8 indicates that the Great Depression created severe economic pressures which forced major companies to cut their research and development expenditure.

    第8段指出，大萧条造成了严重的经济压力，迫使大公司削减研发支出。

  9. The public awareness of the value of research, mentioned in Paragraph 9, was largely created by the wartime achievements in developing radar and the atomic bomb.

    第9段提到，公众对研究价值的认识很大程度上源于战时雷达和原子弹研发取得的成就。

  10. According to Paragraph 10, U.S. research and development has stressed aircraft, defense, space, electronics, and computers, from which its industry has indirectly benefited.

    根据第10段，美国的研究与开发重点关注飞机、国防、航天、电子和计算机，其工业也从中间接受益。

  11. Finally, Paragraph 11 cites Japan’s success in consumer electronics as a prime example of excellence in product innovation.

    最后，第11段将日本在消费电子产品领域的成功视为产品创新卓越表现的典型例子。
